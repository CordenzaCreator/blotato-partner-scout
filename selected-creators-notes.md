# Selected REAL creators for the Blotato build video — notes
Prepared 2026-11-19 (America/Chicago). This replaces the fictional example list on camera. The CSV Jaleel types from is `creators-real.csv` in this same folder.

## How the CSV columns were filled (read this before filming)
- **followers** = the verified count from the source named in each paragraph below. Tracker figures are approximate and dated; counts move daily.
- **posts_about_ai_automation** is a SCORING INPUT, not a counted claim: **3 = posts about AI/content automation regularly, 1 = occasionally, 0 = none found.** Nobody counted individual posts. The script turns it into points (3 x 5 = 15 points).
- **has_business_audience** = their viewers are creators, marketers, agencies, or small businesses who could buy a content automation tool.
- **sells_services** = they run an agency, consultancy, or client-service business (so one partner can refer many client customers). "no" means no client-service offer was found in the sources checked, it does not mean they earn nothing.
- **contact** = a public route description only ("website contact page" where a business website is verified to exist, otherwise "DM only"). No email addresses are in the CSV, because no candidate's business email was verified in the sources opened. Do not read any email out on camera.
- Existing Blotato ties are flagged honestly per candidate. Two of the strong picks already carry Blotato affiliate/referral links according to Blotato's own blog. On camera they are best described as partners to activate and expand, not brand-new cold prospects. Nick Saraev, Jack Roberts, and The AI Automators had NO Blotato tie found in that same source, so they are the cleanest new-recruit examples.
